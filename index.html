FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a thatch roof last?

A well-installed and maintained thatch roof typically lasts 15–20+ years, depending on reed quality, pitch, climate exposure and how regularly it's inspected and maintained.

Do you offer fire-retardant treatment?

Yes — we apply professional fire-retardant treatment to new and existing thatch roofs for extra peace of mind and compliance with insurance requirements.

How much does thatching cost?

Every roof is different, so we price based on size, pitch, access and material. Book a free, no-obligation site visit and we'll give you an honest, itemised quote.

Do you repair storm or wind damage?

Absolutely. We regularly repair storm-damaged ridges, edges and patches, and can usually respond quickly to prevent further water damage.

What areas do you service?

We work across Cape Town and the greater Western Cape, from residential homes and estates to commercial and hospitality properties.

Do you offer a workmanship guarantee?

Yes — every installation and repair is backed by our quality workmanship guarantee. We stand behind our craft.
